Put your licensed background music here (mp3, wav, m4a, ogg, flac).

How tracks are chosen
  cold_open.mp3  rise.mp3  flaw.mp3  collapse.mp3  lesson.mp3  outro.mp3
      -> used for that act of the video
  default.mp3 (or any other file)
      -> used for any act without its own track
  Acts that share a track play it continuously; tracks loop with a crossfade.

What you get automatically
  - every track is level-matched, then ducked under the narration
  - music fades between acts and drops out right before the cold-open impact
  - a 120 BPM clock tick is layered under the collapse act (generated, no license needed)

Suggested moods (instrumental, no vocals, steady, around 100-120 BPM)
  cold_open / collapse : dark, tense, pulsing
  rise / lesson        : light corporate / lo-fi
  flaw                 : neutral, minimal
  outro                : reflective, builds then resolves

LICENSING - important
  Use only music you have the right to use on a monetised channel
  (YouTube Audio Library, or a paid library such as Epidemic Sound or Artlist).
  Keep the license receipts. description.txt lists the track file names so you
  can add the credit your license requires.
